# Proposal

## Why

Builders and maintainers deploying the AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM target for BC Awake need an exhaustive, unambiguous Bill of Materials (BOM) specifying all electronic components, connectors, wiring harnesses, and ratings required for the ESP32-CAM module and its anti-oscillation prototype carrier board. Currently, component values and procurement specs are scattered across schematic notes and assembly text without an authoritative BOM catalog or machine-readable CSV export.

## What Changes

- Create a focused, authoritative Bill of Materials document in `docs/bom-esp32cam.md` (or `docs/bom.md`) dedicated strictly to the ESP32-CAM setup, covering:
  - ESP32-CAM prototype carrier board THT components: dual-rail bulk and ceramic decoupling capacitors (`C_PWR1`..`C_PWR4`), TPMS1 input conditioning resistors and filter (`R1`, `R2`, `C1`), 2N7000 MOSFET buffer network (`Q1`, `R3`, `R4`, `C2`), 2× 1×8 2.54mm female sockets (`J_CAM_L`, `J_CAM_R`), and 1×6 terminal header (`J_EXT`).
  - ATX & Motherboard wiring harness components: ATX 24-pin tap (+5VSB, GND, PS_ON#), TPMS1 pin 9 sense lead, and front-panel momentary switch.
  - Microcontroller & programming hardware: AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM module, USB-UART serial adapter (CP2102/FT232R) or ESP32-CAM-MB daughterboard, and flashing jumper.
  - Precise component specifications: package footprint, lead pitch, voltage/power ratings, tolerances, and sourcing search references (DigiKey, Mouser, LCSC, AliExpress).
- Update `scripts/generate_carrier_pcb.py` to automatically generate a structured, machine-readable CSV BOM (`hardware/kicad/esp32cam-carrier/esp32cam-carrier-bom.csv`).
- Cross-link the BOM from `docs/pinout.md` and `docs/esp32cam-carrier-pcb.md`.
